UV rays don't affect everyone the same, skin type plays a significant role in determining safe exposure times.

Complete the quiz to find out your skin type and safe sun exposure times.

The Fitzpatrick scale is the most widely used assessment to categorize skin types by dermatologists. However, it has little consideration for darker skin tones, which makes diagnosing skin conditions more difficult and often leads to under-treatment. For comprehensive information about skin types please consult a dermatologist.
